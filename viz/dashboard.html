The numbers

Reading the data. "Issues" are serious- and critical-impact WCAG failures from axe-core, plus keyboard-focus, 320 px reflow, and broken-link checks. Scan breadth changed: the June 2026 scan covered ~55,000 pages versus ~23,000 earlier, so per-page rates — not raw totals — measure real progress. Blank points mean an audit did not run that scan (focus indicator: three scans; broken links: one scan). Agencies are the 45 entities current at 30 June 2026; historical sites of merged agencies are rolled into their present-day owner. Source: data/clean/ in this repository.
